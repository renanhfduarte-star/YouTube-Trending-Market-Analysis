# 📈 YouTube Trending Videos: Market Analysis Dashboard

## 🎯 Project Overview
This project was developed for the Video Ad Planning team at Sterling & Draper. The objective is to analyze historical data of trending YouTube videos across different global regions to identify which content categories deserve marketing attention and ad investment.

## 💡 Key Business Insights
Based on the data analysis, three main questions were answered to guide the agency's marketing strategies:

1. **Top Global Categories:** **Entertainment** dominates the trending charts globally with 95,008 videos, followed by *People & Blogs* and *Music*.
2. **Market Distribution:** The trending video volume is evenly distributed across the major analyzed markets (US, France, Russia, India) at roughly 21-23% each, while Japan represents a smaller 10.8% share.
3. **US Market vs. Global Behavior:** User behavior in the United States differs significantly from the global average. In the US, *Music* and *Howto & Style* are highly popular, whereas other regions heavily consume *News & Politics*. **Actionable Insight:** Lifestyle and music campaigns have a higher potential ROI in the US market.
