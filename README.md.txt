# 📈 YouTube Trending Videos: Market Analysis Dashboard

This repository contains the data visualization project developed for the Video Ad Planning team at Sterling & Draper. 

## 🎯 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to analyze historical data of trending YouTube videos across different global regions. The interactive dashboard enables stakeholders to identify which content categories deserve marketing attention and ad investment based on regional consumer behavior.

*Dashboard Link:* [View Interactive Dashboard on Tableau Public](https://public.tableau.com/views/YouTube_Trending_Dashboard_Sterling_Draper/MarketAnalysisDashboard?:language=en-US&:sid=&:redirect=auth&:display_count=n&:origin=viz_share_link)

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

*   **Tableau Public:** Data visualization, interactive filtering, and dashboard layout construction.
*   **Python (Pandas):** Initial data profiling and statistical verification.
*   **Data Source:** YouTube trending logs (`trending_by_time.csv`).

## 💡 Key Findings

Based on the data analysis, three main questions were answered to guide the agency's marketing strategies:

| Insight Category | Key Finding |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Top Global Categories** | Entertainment dominates the trending charts globally with 95,008 videos, followed by People & Blogs and Music. |
| **Market Distribution** | The trending video volume is evenly distributed across the major analyzed markets (US, France, Russia, India) at roughly 21-23% each, while Japan represents a smaller 10.8% share. |
| **US Market vs. Global Behavior** | User behavior in the United States differs significantly from the global average. In the US, Music and *How-to & Style* are highly popular, whereas other regions heavily consume *News & Politics*. |

## 🚀 Strategic Recommendations

*   **US Market Allocation:** Lifestyle and music campaigns have a significantly higher potential ROI in the US market. Ad spend should be heavily directed toward these categories rather than News & Politics when targeting American audiences.
